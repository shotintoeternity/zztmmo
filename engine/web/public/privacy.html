ZZTMMO Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

ZZTMMO (zztmmo.com) is a multiplayer, browser version of the 1991 game ZZT. This page says what it keeps about you, why, and how to get it back or have it removed. You can play without signing in.

If you play as a guest

  • The name you type is shown to other players in the world you join, in chat, and in replays.
  • Your settings (player colour, comfort settings, which tips you have seen) stay in your own browser's storage. They are not sent to us except as needed to show your colour to other players.
  • Chat lines you send are kept on the server with the name you used, and shown to other players.
  • Saved games you make are kept on the server under the save name you choose.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. When you do, Google tells us your account ID, email address and the name on your Google account, and we keep:

  • A sign-in record: your Google account ID, email address, the name on your Google account, and when you first and last signed in.
  • Your username, which you choose. It is the only name other players see. The name on your Google account and your email are never shown to other players.
  • Your profile (if you fill it in), your settings, and the players you follow or block.
  • Your progress in each world, so you can carry on where you left off.
  • Chat lines you send, challenge times and their replays, and worlds you own (ones you published in the editor or dreamed).

We ask Google only for basic profile information (openid, email, profile). We cannot see your Google password or anything else in your Google account.

What everyone shares

  • Replays. Play sessions are recorded so they can be watched back. A recording holds the moves made and the names players were shown by.
  • Dreamed worlds. If you use "Dream a world", the idea you type is sent to Anthropic's Claude to build the world. Don't put personal information in it.
  • Your IP address is used to limit how often requests can be made and may appear in server logs.

Cookies and browser storage

  • Signing in sets a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and a short-lived cookie used only while you sign in.
  • The game keeps your settings, and a note that you have visited before, in your browser's local storage.
  • There is no advertising and no third-party tracking.

What we do with it

We use this information only to run the game: to show who is playing, keep your progress and settings, run leaderboards and replays, and keep the game safe (moderators can mute, remove or refuse an account). We do not sell it or share it with anyone, apart from the services the game runs on: Amazon Web Services hosts the server, Google handles sign-in, and Anthropic builds dreamed worlds.

Getting your data, or deleting it

  • Download it: sign in, open the Account menu (G on the title screen) and choose Export my data. You get one file of everything kept about your account.
  • Delete your account: in the same menu choose Delete my account and type your username to confirm. Your sign-in record, username, profile, settings, follows and blocks, and progress are removed straight away, and your username becomes free for others.
  • So that shared things still make sense to other players, your past chat lines stay but are shown as "(deleted player)", your challenge times and replays stay under that name, and worlds you owned stay playable with no owner.
  • If a moderator refused your account, that record is kept, so deleting an account cannot undo a ban.
  • The server is backed up daily and backups are kept for 14 days, so deleted information can remain in a backup for up to two weeks before it is gone for good.

Children

ZZTMMO is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
