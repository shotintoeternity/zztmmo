# Provenance and third-party content

## ZZTMMO

This client speaks the protocol of, and reuses code from,
[shotintoeternity/zztmmo](https://github.com/shotintoeternity/zztmmo), MIT
licensed. `src/textwindow.ts` and `src/sidebar.ts` are copied from its
`engine/web/src` (with the chat and players rows of the sidebar replaced), and
`src/input.ts`, `src/modals.ts`, `src/protocol.ts` and `src/net.ts` are
transcribed from its `keys.ts`, `modal.ts` and `main.ts`.

## The font

`src/pc_ega.png` is the 8x14 EGA font sheet from Adrian Siekierka's
[Zeta](https://github.com/asiekierka/zeta) (`fonts/pc_ega.png`), by way of
ZZTMMO, whose renderer draws with the same sheet.

## ZZT

ZZT was created by Tim Sweeney and published by Epic MegaGames in 1991. This
repository redistributes no Epic content; the worlds are served by the ZZTMMO
server this client connects to.
